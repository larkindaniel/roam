## Roam additions and structural rules

1. **Experience category:** one MapKit place per experience; seven ranking categories: Food & Drink, Outdoors, Day Trips, Culture, Nightlife, Activities, Stays. A locality can represent a day trip. A list may contain many experiences, but never turns into a single multipin rated experience.
2. **City detail:** personal and global derived score; category breakdown, sample size, unique visitor count/confidence context and links to contributing experiences. This is a confirmed product direction without a captured Beli equivalent. Numerical formula stays deferred.
3. **Profile globe:** pins, shaded visited countries, unique city/country counts, expanded view and place navigation on own and other profiles. Expanded globe interactions are a proposed Roam design; source capture is a flat Beli map.
4. **Public graph:** profiles and follows are public. Owner credentials and personal notes are not profile content. Blocks/mutes do not require private profiles.
5. **Ranking vs presentation:** guides and global discovery can have editorial/aggregate order. Personal category rankings are separate. Proposed All view groups by category or defaults to date; a cross-category numeric score sort needs an explicit comparability warning and approval.

## Proposed release decisions requiring confirmation

Core draft coverage includes tracking/ranking, discovery, social activity, free custom lists/guides, sharing, shared wants, profile globe and city scoring. Do not reduce this to place search plus saved pins.

Proposed deferrals: paid creator subscriptions/billing; restaurant reservation/ordering/calendar/Gmail integrations; referral-gated scores; predictive taste matching; school/company leaderboards; automatic camera-roll place extraction; provider-specific imports beyond paste-first. Goals/streaks, travel-history calendar, challenges, generated guides and collaborative guides remain documented candidates; their release boundary is open, not silently removed.

No row marked deferred may disappear from implementation planning without an accepted decision recorded against its B/R IDs. Pending captures may change a design, but cannot override confirmed product decisions.
