## Privacy policy draft

**Roam Privacy Policy**

Effective date: [publication date]

Contact: [public support address or form URL]

Roam helps you remember places you have visited, plan trips, and share selected
experiences with friends. Daniel Larkin operates Roam. This policy describes the
information Roam handles and the choices you have in the app.

### Information you give Roam

When you create an account, Roam stores your email address or Apple sign-in
identity, a user ID, and the profile details you choose, such as your name,
handle, bio, avatar, and home city. Roam stores the places, visits, dates,
ratings, notes, photos, saved places, lists, trips, and taste answers you add.
It also stores social actions such as friend requests, trip membership, direct
shares, likes, comments, posts, blocks, mutes, and reports.

Roam uses this information to run your account, show your travel history and
plans, share content with the audience you choose, and handle safety reports.
Your email and sign-in details are not shown on your public profile. Other
members can see profile details and any content you choose to share with them.
Visits can be set to Only me, Friends, or Public. Review the audience before
posting; the app's current default for a new visit is Public.

### Location and photos

If you grant location access, Roam uses your device's location for nearby
search and distance sorting. The app works without location access. Places you
choose to save or log have location details such as place names and coordinates
that Roam stores with your travel records. The system photo picker lets you
choose images; Roam removes image location and other embedded metadata before
uploading them.

### Services and optional AI handoff

Roam uses Supabase to host accounts, database records, and photos; Apple MapKit
for maps and place search; and Google Places and Wikipedia for some place
details and images. Roam does not include an advertising SDK in the current
build. The Plan with AI screen prepares a prompt from your tastes and liked
places on your device. You can edit and copy it. Opening ChatGPT or Claude
copies the prompt to your clipboard; those services receive it only if you
choose to paste or submit it there, under their own policies.

### Your controls and deletion

You can edit or delete visits and many profile details, choose each visit's
audience, and block other members. Settings includes Delete account. A deletion
request hides your profile and ends your sessions immediately; a background job
then removes your account, visits, photos, social activity, and other data.
Reports you filed remain as moderation records without your account identity.
Text already copied or shared outside Roam cannot be recalled.

To ask about your information or report a privacy concern, use [public support
address or form URL].

## Support page draft

**Roam Support**

For account, sign-in, privacy, or safety help, contact Daniel Larkin at
[public support address or form URL]. Include your Roam handle and a short
description of the issue, but do not send passwords or Apple authorization
codes. You can report a place, post, comment, photo, or member inside the app;
Settings shows the status of reports you have filed. Daniel reviews incoming
reports and may remove content or restrict accounts when needed.

## Terms page draft

**Roam Terms of Use**

Effective date: [publication date]

Use Roam to record and share your own travel experiences. Do not post content
you lack the right to share, another person's private information, spam,
harassment, threats, or illegal material. You keep ownership of your content
and give Roam permission to display it to the audience you select while it
remains in the service. You can edit or delete your content in the app and can
delete your account in Settings. Roam may remove content or restrict accounts
to protect members and operate the service. Contact [public support address or
form URL] about these terms or a moderation decision.
