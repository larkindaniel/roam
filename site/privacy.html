Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Roam: Places & Memories ("Roam", "we", "us") helps you remember places you've been, plan trips and share selected experiences with friends. Roam is operated by Daniel Larkin in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what information Roam handles, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions go to dxxxx441@gmail.com.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our hosting provider), or your Sign in with Apple identity, and a user ID.
  • Profile: the name, handle, bio, photo and home cities you choose.
  • Your travel content: places, visits, dates, ratings and rankings, notes, photos, saved places, lists, trips and optional taste answers.
  • Social activity: friend requests and friendships, trip membership, places you send to friends, likes, comments, posts, and blocks, mutes and reports.
  • Settings: preferences such as distance units, haptics and in-app notification choices.

Information from your device

  • Location (optional): if you allow it, Roam uses your location while the app is open to search and sort places near you. Roam works without it. We don't track your location in the background.
  • Photos you choose: Roam uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you select. Location and other embedded metadata are removed on your device before upload.
  • Technical data: like any internet service, our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request information when the app connects to it.

Roam does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not sell your information.

How we use information

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your travel history, rankings and plans.
  • To personalize suggestions: Roam suggests places and cities from the places you've rated and saved, the kinds of places you tend to like, and what your friends have visited, loved, listed or sent you. Only activity you're allowed to see counts, so Only me visits and blocked people are never used. Ratings shared with Everyone can also count toward places being popular on Roam.
  • To share what you post with the audience you choose.
  • To keep Roam safe: handle reports, enforce our Terms of Use and respond to misuse.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

Who can see what

Anyone signed in to Roam can see your name, handle, photo, bio and wishlist. Each visit, with its rating, note and photos, goes only to the audience you pick: Only me, Friends or Everyone. New visits start as Friends. Lists stay private until you share them. Your email address, sign-in details, settings, reports, and who you've blocked or muted are never shown to other members. People you block can't see your visits.

Roam doesn't read the contacts on your phone. Your friends are the people you add in Roam; that list is stored with your account and used to share visits and personalize suggestions.

Service providers

We use these providers to run Roam. They process information only to provide their service to us or to you:

  • Supabase hosts accounts, the database and photos (servers in the United States).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, maps and place search (MapKit). Map and search requests go from your device to Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Places supplies some place details and photos. Our server sends Google the place's name and location, not your identity.
  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) supplies some city and place images, requested directly from your device.

Your information may be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

Plan with AI

Plan with AI builds a trip-planning prompt on your device from your taste answers and places you liked. You can edit and copy it, and open ChatGPT or Claude. Nothing is sent to those services unless you paste or submit it there yourself, under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can edit or delete visits, photos, lists and profile details at any time. Settings › Delete account hides your profile and signs you out immediately, then permanently deletes your account, visits, photos, lists, friends, likes, comments, posts and other data. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke Roam's access to your Apple sign-in. Reports you filed are kept as moderation records without your identity. Short-lived server logs and backups held by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule. Anything you or others already copied or shared outside Roam can't be recalled.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, or to object to some uses. Most of this can be done in the app. For anything else, email dxxxx441@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local privacy regulator (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner).

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and access rules on our database limit each member to what they're allowed to see. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Roam is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above, and we'll tell you in the app before significant changes take effect.
