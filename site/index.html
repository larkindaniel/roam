Roam Support

Help with Roam: Places & Memories for iPhone.

Contact us

Email dxxxx441@gmail.com for account, sign-in, privacy or safety help, or to ask about a moderation decision. Include your Roam handle and a short description of the problem. Never send your password or any Apple sign-in code.

We aim to reply within a few days.

Reporting content or a person

Use Report on any place, post, comment, photo or profile in the app. You can see the status of reports you've filed in Settings › Your reports. Reports are reviewed by a person, and content or accounts that break the Terms of Use may be removed.

Blocking and muting

Open someone's profile to block or mute them. Blocked people can't see your visits. You can undo either in Settings › Blocked people or Muted people.

Passwords and sign-in

If you signed up with email and forgot your password, choose Forgot password? on the email sign-in screen to get a reset code. If you use Sign in with Apple, your sign-in is managed by your Apple Account.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings › Delete account. Your profile is hidden immediately, and your visits, photos, lists, friends and other data are then permanently deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details.
